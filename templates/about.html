About Market Digest

Learn the market in 5 minutes a day.

The best finance shows are long, fast and full of jargon. Market Digest listens to them for you and turns each one into a short, plain-English study page, with the market data beside it.

Read today’s digest →

Why it exists

Too much to follow

Hours of podcasts and hundreds of headlines every day. Nobody learning to invest has time for all of it.

Too much jargon

Basis points, convertible notes, P/E ratios… Every term is explained in plain English, with an example, and saved to a growing glossary.

Too much noise

No hype channels or stock tips. Only hosts and newsrooms with real credentials, with opinions clearly separated from facts.

How it works

  1. 1

    Find

    Every morning we check {{ n_podcasts }} hand-picked podcasts and {{ n_feeds }} newsroom feeds for anything new.

  2. 2

    Listen

    Each new episode is transcribed word for word by an open-source speech model (Whisper).

  3. 3

    Understand

    An AI model reads the transcript like a teacher and writes the key points, the important numbers, the lesson and the jargon to know. It is told to use only what the source said, to keep forecasts and opinions separate, and to flag low-value episodes instead of summarizing them.

  4. 4

    Connect

    Headlines from across the newsrooms are grouped into the day’s hottest stories, and prices, charts and headlines are added for the companies involved.

  5. 5

    Publish

    Everything becomes one daily page, archived forever, with a searchable glossary of every term so far.

What to keep in mind

  • It’s AI-written. Summaries are checked for accuracy, but mistakes can happen. Every story links to the original, so check it before relying on a number.
  • It’s for learning, not advice. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
  • Credit goes to the creators. We publish short summaries in our own words, never transcripts or full articles, and always link back. If you enjoy a show, listen to it or subscribe. Market Digest is not affiliated with any show, newsroom or company mentioned.
  • Market data comes from Yahoo Finance, is a snapshot from the morning run, and may be delayed.
  • Creators: if you’d like your show removed, open a request and we’ll take it down promptly.
